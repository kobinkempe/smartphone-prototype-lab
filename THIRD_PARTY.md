# Asset and dependency provenance

The Zoom and Scroll prototypes include Three.js distributions. Their notices are in [Zoom's vendor license](site/zoom/vendor/LICENSE) and [Scroll's Three.js license](site/scroll/THREE-LICENSE.txt). The Stack prototype bundles DM Sans and Libre Caslon Display fonts with their [DM Sans](site/stack/fonts/dm-sans-OFL.txt) and [Libre Caslon](site/stack/fonts/libre-caslon-OFL.txt) notices. Preserve these notices when reusing those assets.

Educational figures are conceptual illustrations; the encyclopedia artwork's generation provenance is in [docs/ILLUSTRATION.md](docs/ILLUSTRATION.md). Source links for the scientific explanations are retained in [docs/RESEARCH.md](docs/RESEARCH.md), [docs/MAGNET-RESEARCH.md](docs/MAGNET-RESEARCH.md) and the prototype pages. External web fonts may require a network connection; fallback fonts remain available.

The diagrams explain concepts and interface flows. They are not validated fabrication instructions or a complete phone manufacturing plan. Marked stand-in pages remain incomplete.
