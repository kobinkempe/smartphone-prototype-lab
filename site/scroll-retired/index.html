Why insulated copper? +

Copper carries current. Insulation separates neighboring turns so the wire remains one intended electrical path. The compliant surround supports the diaphragm while allowing movement.

03ASSEMBLY STEP

Center it.
Then seal it.

Put the moving coil in the magnetic gap, with clearance on both sides.

The centers the coil while the bonds the surround to the frame. Remove the temporary fixture when the assembly is secured.

THE KEY IDEA

A centered coil can move freely. Contact with the fixed iron can cause rubbing and unwanted noise.

What is being sealed? +

The diaphragm’s outer surround attaches to the frame. The rear enclosure defines the acoustic loading behind it. The coil itself is free to move in the gap; it is not glued to the magnet.

How does the force change? +

Changing current in the voice coil changes the electrodynamic force. The coil and diaphragm move together; the permanent magnet stays fixed. This visual slows and exaggerates the motion. Real speakers also have mechanical and thermal limits.
